QUERY 6 - Books with their categories
============================================================
SQL:
SELECT
            books.title,
            books.price_gbp,
            books.rating,
            categories.category_name
        FROM books
        JOIN categories
            ON books.category_id = categories.category_id
        ORDER BY books.price_gbp DESC
        LIMIT 10
